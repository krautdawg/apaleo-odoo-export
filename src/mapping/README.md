# src/mapping

Account mapping: the editable CSV from apaleo accounts to Odoo account codes with the Odoo tax and its rate. PMS-neutral; must not import from `src/sources/` (enforced by lint).

- `load.ts` reads a mapping file (UTF-8 or CP1252, `;` or `,`, decimal comma or point, account codes and taxes kept as text) and rejects Abacus, BMD and DATEV mappings, the account exports of paid connectors, duplicate keys and forbidden wildcards.
- `resolve.ts` finds the row for one side of a ledger line (exact, any type, parent chain, rate wildcard, plain wildcard).
- `check.ts` validates rows (`checkMapping`: account code and tax format, taxes on payments and receivables, the known l10n_de taxes and their rates, Kz 45, German rates, accounts with a default tax or generated by Odoo) and the accounts booked in a period (`checkCoverage`, `E-UNMAPPED`); `checkUsedTaxes` repeats the tax warnings for the rows a month uses.
- `l10n-de.ts` lists what the tool knows about Odoo's l10n_de: the sale taxes with rate and Kennzahl, the template accounts with a default tax, and the Kasse and Geldtransit codes of a fresh database per chart and Odoo version.
- `template.ts` applies the hotel rules in `templates/skr03-hotel.rules.csv` and `templates/skr04-hotel.rules.csv` to a chart of accounts, with the generated accounts of `odoo.version`.
- `file.ts` writes mapping files and appends rows for `init-mapping --update`.
